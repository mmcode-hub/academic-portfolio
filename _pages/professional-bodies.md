# Professional Memberships and Academic Responsibilities

Throughout my academic career, I have actively contributed to professional organisations, university academic bodies, institutional governance, curriculum development and academic evaluation. These responsibilities provided opportunities to contribute to higher education and the development of Computer Science and Information Technology programmes.

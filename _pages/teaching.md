# Teaching Philosophy and Experience

## My Teaching Philosophy

Teaching is my passion, and I strongly believe in sharing knowledge and inspiring students to explore the world of Computer Science. Throughout my academic career, I have enjoyed introducing students to fundamental computing concepts, programming techniques and emerging technologies.

Computer Science is a rapidly evolving discipline. To remain relevant and effective, one must continuously update one's knowledge and adapt to technological transformations. I strongly believe in the philosophy of **"Learn, Unlearn and Relearn"**. This has been my guiding motto throughout my teaching career.

My approach to teaching combines strong fundamentals, practical programming experience and exposure to emerging technologies. I believe that students should not merely learn to use technology but should also understand the concepts and principles behind it.

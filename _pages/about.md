## Welcome

Dr. Muralidharan Murugesan is an educator, researcher, and software developer
with 38 years of distinguished service in higher education. 
A retired Associate Professor and former Head of the Department of Computer Science 
at Nehru Memorial College (Puthanampatti, Trichy).
He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Bharathidasan University.

Throughout a career spanning early microcomputing to contemporary multi-core architectures,
Dr. Muralidharan played a foundational role in establishing
the college’s computer science department and modern laboratory facilities.
His teaching spans Artificial Intelligence, Operating Systems, Computer Graphics, and Python programming, 
alongside initiatives in digital courseware and institutional governance—contributing 
significantly to the college’s autonomous status and NAAC accreditation.

Dr. Muralidharan's core research areas include Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Web Mining,
and he is the author of a textbook on Python. 
A committed community builder, he was recognized as the State Best NSS Programme Officer 
and remains actively involved in organizing open-source and Python community conferences. 
Complementing his technical pursuits, he is an avid heritage photographer 
with a deep focus on ancient temple architecture.

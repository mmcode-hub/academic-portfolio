## Professional Profile

Dr. Muralidharan Murugesan is an academician, researcher and software developer with 38 years of distinguished service in higher education. He is a retired Associate Professor of Computer Science and former Head of the Department of Computer Science at Nehru Memorial College, Puthanampatti, Tiruchirappalli. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Bharathidasan University.

His professional journey spans the evolution of computing from the early days of microcomputers to contemporary multi-core architectures and emerging technologies.

## Academic and Professional Contributions

During his academic career, he played an important role in establishing and developing the Department of Computer Science and modern computer laboratory facilities at his institution.

His teaching experience encompasses several areas of Computer Science, including:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Operating Systems
- Computer Graphics
- Python Programming

He also contributed to digital courseware development, institutional governance and academic administration. His institutional contributions supported the college's autonomous status and NAAC accreditation.

## Research and Publications

His major research interests include:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Web Mining

He has authored a textbook on Python and continues to take an interest in emerging developments in Computer Science and technology.

## Community Engagement and Recognition

Beyond his academic responsibilities, Dr. Muralidharan has actively contributed to student development and professional communities.

He was recognised as the State Best NSS Programme Officer for his contributions to the National Service Scheme. He has also been involved in organising open-source and Python community conferences, encouraging knowledge sharing and collaborative learning.

## Personal Interests

Apart from his academic and technical interests, he is an avid heritage photographer with a particular interest in ancient temples, their architecture, history and archaeological significance.

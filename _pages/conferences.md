# Seminars, Conferences, Workshops and Training Programmes

Throughout my academic career, I have participated in national and international conferences, seminars, workshops and specialised training programmes. These activities have provided opportunities to exchange academic knowledge, acquire new skills and remain updated with developments in Computer Science, Information Technology, research methodology and higher education.
